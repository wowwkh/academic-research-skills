Dear Editors,

Please consider our Review Article, “Low-grade inflammation, stress-axis dysregulation and a proposed immune-metabolic continuum in suboptimal health defined by SHSQ-25,” for publication in *Molecular Psychiatry*.

Suboptimal health status is commonly assessed with the SHSQ-25, yet biological reports use non-equivalent thresholds, populations, matrices and estimands. This review provides a source-audited narrative synthesis rather than a forced meta-analysis. Three direct SHS-related cortisol reports are retained study by study, but no primary pooled direct-cortisol estimate is reported because cohort independence, exposure definitions and estimands cannot be shown to be sufficiently comparable. Low-grade-inflammation, quality-of-life and fatigue findings are kept as bridge evidence. A proposed SLIM framework organizes immune-metabolic and mitochondrial hypotheses while explicitly distinguishing mechanistic-transfer evidence from direct human SHS evidence.

The principal safeguards of the manuscript are:

- 19 identity-verified Vancouver references with citation order audited;
- three labelled main-text tables and no final figure in the current package;
- an explicit direct/bridge/transfer evidence hierarchy;
- no standalone biomarker diagnostic claim and no causal claim from cross-sectional evidence; and
- transparent quarantine of unresolved or scope-excluded records and exploratory quantitative outputs.

The work is intended as a narrative Review Article and does not report a new participant-level dataset or a new human intervention. The final data-availability, code-availability and ethics wording will be confirmed in the submission package.
