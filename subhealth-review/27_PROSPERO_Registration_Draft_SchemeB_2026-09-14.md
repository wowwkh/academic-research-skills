## 7 Review question
PICOS:
- Population: Adults with suboptimal health defined by SHSQ-25 ≥33/35 persisting ≥3 months without organic disease, or healthy population with low-grade inflammation CRP 3-10 mg/L + IL-6 3.25-20 pg/ml plus fatigue/HRQoL (concept-homologous bridge)
- Intervention/Exposure: Chronic psychological stress, low-grade inflammation, immune-metabolic reprogramming
- Comparator: Healthy controls SHSQ-25 <33, or reference CRP <1 + IL-6 <1.5, or healthy PBMC/HRV/NLR
- Outcome: Primary cortisol 205.8 vs 161.8 ng/ml, NLR, HRV SDNN/RMSSD, SF-36 vitality, fatigue risk, OXPHOS Seahorse, secondary mtDNA, Drp1 Ser616/Ser637, Mfn2/OPA1, lactate/succinate, cGAS-STING IFN-β CXCL10, NLRP3 IL-1β
- Study design: Observational cross-sectional, cohort, validation, mechanistic in vitro/animal for pathway depth, reviews for framework

## 9 Types of study to be included
- Inclusion: SHSQ-25-defined SHS with objective markers cortisol/NLR/HRV/endothelial/MDA/SOD/Seahorse/mtDNA/Drp1, or population low-grade CRP 3-10 + IL-6 3.25-20 + fatigue/HRQoL SF-36, or mechanistic immunometabolism/mitochondrial dynamics with phosphorylation sites, or validation multi-ethnic SHSQ-25
