# Claim-to-source traceability matrix — Scheme B — 2026-10-15

## Purpose

This matrix is a claim-level safety layer for the source-audited narrative manuscript. It maps permitted manuscript statements to the identity-verified anchors in `50_Verified_Reference_Register_SchemeB_2026-10-15.md` and records the wording that must not be used. It does not add literature or create effect estimates.
