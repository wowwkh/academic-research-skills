# Mechanistic transfer-evidence matrix for the SLIM model — Scheme B — 2026-10-08

## Purpose

The source-audited human SHS records do not directly measure mitochondrial dynamics, immune-cell metabolic flux, cGAS–STING or NLRP3. The following verified articles are therefore classified as **mechanistic transfer evidence**, not direct SHS evidence. They support biological plausibility and experimental design, but cannot be used to claim that the same pathway has been demonstrated in SHSQ-25-defined SHS.

## Verified mechanistic anchors

### M1 — Immune-cell metabolic rewiring

**Exact title:** *Immunometabolism governs dendritic cell and macrophage function*
**PMID/PubMed:** [26694970](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26694970/)
**DOI:** [10.1084/jem.20151570](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20151570)

**Transferable point:** immune signals can reprogram glycolysis, TCA-cycle intermediates and oxidative phosphorylation, with succinate/HIF-1α and glycolytic pathways participating in inflammatory effector programs.
**Boundary:** general immune-cell biology; not an SHSQ-25 cohort and not evidence for a specific SHS threshold.

### M2 — MFN2/OPA1, bioenergetics and quality control

**Exact title:** *The Balance of MFN2 and OPA1 in Mitochondrial Dynamics, Cellular Homeostasis, and Disease*
**PMID/PubMed:** [40149969](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40149969/)
**DOI:** [10.3390/biom15030433](https://doi.org/10.3390/biom15030433)

**Transferable point:** mitochondrial fusion regulators MFN2 and OPA1 influence network structure, oxidative phosphorylation, bioenergetics and mitophagy.
**Boundary:** mechanistic review focused on cellular and disease models; it does not establish MFN2/OPA1 abnormalities in SHS.

### M3 — Drp1 phosphorylation-site depth

**Exact title:** *The Phosphorylation Status of Drp1-Ser637 by PKA in Mitochondrial Fission Modulates Mitophagy via PINK1/Parkin to Exert Multipolar Spindles Assembly during Mitosis*
**PMID/PubMed:** [33805672](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33805672/)
**DOI:** [10.3390/biom11030424](https://doi.org/10.3390/biom11030424)

**Transferable point:** Drp1 Ser637/Ser616 state and PKA/PINK1/Parkin context can be linked to mitochondrial fission and mitophagy in a cell model.
**Boundary:** the study is not an SHS study; phosphorylation-site effects are context-dependent and cannot be reduced to a universal “Ser616 up/Ser637 down” rule.

### M4 — Mitochondrial DNA and cGAS–STING

**Exact title:** *Mitochondrial DNA Leakage and cGas/STING Pathway in Microglia: Crosstalk Between Neuroinflammation and Neurodegeneration*
**PMID/PubMed:** [38685462](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38685462/)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neuroscience.2024.04.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroscience.2024.04.009)

**Transferable point:** mitochondrial oxidative stress and cytosolic mtDNA can activate cGAS–STING and promote inflammatory cytokines in microglial contexts.
**Boundary:** neuroinflammation review; it is not direct evidence from SHSQ-25-defined participants.

### M5 — Mitochondrial damage as a cGAS–STING trigger

**Exact title:** *Mitochondrial Damage Causes Inflammation via cGAS-STING Signaling in Acute Kidney Injury*
**PMID/PubMed:** [31665638](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31665638/)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.celrep.2019.09.050](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.09.050)

**Transferable point:** mitochondrial damage and mtDNA leakage activated cGAS–STING in an acute-kidney-injury animal/cell model, and STING deficiency reduced inflammation.
**Boundary:** disease-specific animal and cell model; it cannot establish a causal pathway in mild human SHS.

### M6 — Oxidized mtDNA and NLRP3

**Exact title:** *Oxidized mitochondrial DNA activates the NLRP3 inflammasome during apoptosis*
**PMID/PubMed:** [22342844](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22342844/)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.immuni.2012.01.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2012.01.009)

**Transferable point:** oxidized mtDNA released during mitochondrial apoptosis activated NLRP3 and promoted IL-1β production in experimental systems.
**Boundary:** experimental apoptosis/inflammasome biology; no direct SHS or human low-grade-inflammation estimate.

## Required manuscript boundary

These anchors may support the rationale and roadmap sections of the narrative manuscript. They must not be added to the direct SHS Meta input, the source-audited GRADE direct-cortisol judgment, or any claim that mitochondrial dynamics has already been observed in SHSQ-25-defined participants.
