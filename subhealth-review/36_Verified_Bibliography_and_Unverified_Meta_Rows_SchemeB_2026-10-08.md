# Verified bibliography and unverified Meta rows — Scheme B — 2026-10-08

## Why this correction is necessary

The previous request list contained working labels and several unverified DOI-looking strings. They must not be treated as real citations. This file separates records that have a traceable PubMed/DOI identity from Meta rows that currently have no defensible bibliographic identity.

## Verified records to locate and download

### Direct SHS/cortisol and SHSQ-25 records

1. **Development and Evaluation of a Questionnaire for Measuring Suboptimal Health Status in Urban Chinese**  
   Yan YX, Liu YQ, Li M, et al. *Journal of Epidemiology*. 2009;19(6):333-341.  
   **PMID:** 19749497  
   **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.2188/jea.je20080086  
   **Use:** SHSQ-25 origin, definition, cutoff framework; not a cortisol Meta row.

2. **Association of Suboptimal Health Status and Cardiovascular Risk Factors in Urban Chinese Workers**  
   Yan YX, Dong J, Liu YQ, et al. *Journal of Urban Health*. 2012;89(2):329-338.  
   **PMID:** 22203493  
   **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11524-011-9636-8  
   **Use:** direct SHS/cardiovascular risk study; reports cortisol and may provide a legitimate replacement for one provisional cortisol row.

3. **Association of suboptimal health status with psychosocial stress, plasma cortisol and mRNA expression of glucocorticoid receptor α/β in lymphocyte**  
   Yan YX, Dong J, Liu YQ, et al. *Stress*. 2015;18(1):29-34.  
   **PMID:** [25518867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25518867/)  
   **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3109/10253890.2014.999233  
   **Use:** direct SHS/plasma cortisol and glucocorticoid-receptor study.

4. **Latent class analysis to evaluate performance of plasma cortisol, plasma catecholamines, and SHSQ-25 for early recognition of suboptimal health status**  
   Yan YX, Wu LJ, Xiao HB, et al. *EPMA Journal*. 2018;9(3):299-305.  
   **PMID:** 30174765; **PMCID:** PMC6107457  
   **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s13167-018-0144-8  
   **Use:** exact source of the 205.80±29.82 vs 161.80±7.79 ng/mL cortisol values.
