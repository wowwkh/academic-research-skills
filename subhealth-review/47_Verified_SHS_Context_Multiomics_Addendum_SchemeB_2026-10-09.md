# Verified SHS context and multi-omics addendum — Scheme B — 2026-10-09

## Scope

This addendum expands the narrative evidence map with four exact-identity SHS records identified through PubMed/PMC verification. They are context or mechanistic-roadmap evidence, not additions to the direct cortisol Meta. Their quantitative results are not pooled because they address different populations, outcomes and estimands.

## Verified sources

### C1 — Endothelial dysfunction and SHS

**Exact title:** *Integration of suboptimal health status and endothelial dysfunction as a new aspect for risk evaluation of cardiovascular disease*
**PMID/PubMed:** [27621756](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27621756/)
**DOI:** [10.1186/s13167-016-0068-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13167-016-0068-0)

A community-based cross-sectional study of 459 residents in Samara, Russia, combined SHSQ-25 with endothelial function and cardiovascular risk measures. The reported correlations between endothelial function and overall SHS score, fatigue and cardiovascular domains support an objective vascular bridge, but not a direct cortisol or mitochondrial estimate.

### C2 — Glycomic multi-omics

**Exact title:** *Glycomic biomarkers are instrumental for suboptimal health status management in the context of predictive, preventive, and personalized medicine*
**PMID/PubMed:** [35719133](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35719133/)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s13167-022-00278-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13167-022-00278-1)

A case-control study compared 124 SHS participants with 124 matched healthy controls using plasma IgG N-glycan profiling. The reported analysis identified multiple SHS-associated glycan traits after adjustment and linked selected glycan features to SHSQ-25 domains. This is relevant multi-omics context and a candidate biomarker layer, but it is not evidence for CRP/IL-6, cortisol or mitochondrial dynamics.

### C3 — SHSQ-25 network structure

**Exact title:** *Conceptualised psycho-medical footprint for health status outcomes and the potential impacts for early detection and prevention of chronic diseases in the context of 3P medicine*
**PMID/PubMed:** [38094584](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38094584/)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s13167-023-00344-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13167-023-00344-2)

A cross-sectional study of 217 healthy Ghanaian participants used median SHSQ-25 grouping and graphical LASSO/network methods. The optimal and SHS networks differed in connectivity and structure. This supports a systems-level symptom architecture, but it is not a longitudinal causal network and does not provide direct immune-metabolic measurements.

### C4 — Caregiving and SHS risk

**Exact title:** *The caregiving role influences Suboptimal Health Status and psychological symptoms in unpaid carers*
**PMID/PubMed:** [39239105](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39239105/)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s13167-024-00370-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13167-024-00370-8)

An Australian cross-sectional study included 203 unpaid carers and 165 general-population controls. SHSQ-25-defined SHS was more common among carers, and the reported adjusted association was aOR 6.400 (95% CI 3.751–10.919). This supports psychosocial exposure as a candidate upstream stratum, but the study does not establish cortisol, low-grade inflammation or mitochondrial mediation.

## Synthesis role

| Record | Evidence layer | Appropriate use | Not appropriate for |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | SHS + vascular function | Objective bridge and future composite endpoint | Direct cortisol Meta or mitochondrial causal claim |
| C2 | SHS + glycomic multi-omics | Multi-omic biomarker roadmap | Pooling with cortisol, NLR or OXPHOS |
| C3 | SHS symptom-network structure | Systems-level phenotype description | Causal temporal inference |
| C4 | SHS + psychosocial exposure | Upstream risk-stratification context | Mediation claim without longitudinal biomarkers |

These four records strengthen the proposed evidence architecture—phenotype, vascular function, multi-omics and psychosocial exposure—but do not alter the locked primary decision: the current direct SHS-cortisol synthesis remains narrative-only and no new pooled estimate is generated.
